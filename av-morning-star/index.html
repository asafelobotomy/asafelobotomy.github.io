AV Morning Star

Download video and audio from YouTube, Odysee and 1000+ sites.

Download v0.4.2Source on GitHubSupport

What it does

AV Morning Star wraps yt-dlp in a clean desktop app. Basic mode is one click; Advanced mode adds loudness normalisation, denoising, stabilisation and container choice. YouTube cookies are only read with your consent, and only in memory.

Screenshots

Features

Any qualityBest, 4K down to 360p, plus audio-only in MP3, FLAC, Opus and more.
Playlists & channelsFetch every entry, pick the ones you want.
Cookieless by defaultAsks before reading a browser's cookies; never writes them to disk.
Audio cleanupEBU R128 loudness, dynamic normalisation, FFT noise reduction.
Video cleanuphqdn3d denoise, deshake stabilisation, unsharp sharpening.
Filename templatesClick tags like Title, Uploader and Quality to build names.
