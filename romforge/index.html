romforge

Identify, tidy, enrich and play your ROM collection, from the desk or the sofa.

Download v0.15.0Source on GitHubSupport

What it does

romforge works out exactly what each file is by its fingerprint, gives it the right name and art, and starts it in the right emulator. Scanning only reads. Nothing is moved, renamed or repacked until you've seen the plan and said yes, and every change can be undone.

Features

Identifies every gameMatches against No-Intro, Redump, TOSEC, MAME and ScummVM by hash, even inside zip, 7z and CHD.
Tidies your collectionRenames and repacks into one folder (zip for cartridges, CHD for discs), previewed first, one-click undo.
Fills in the detailsBox art, screenshots, fanart, logos and release data from free open sources.
Plays themRetroArch or the right standalone emulator: DuckStation, PCSX2, Dolphin, PPSSPP, MAME and more.
Feels like a consoleGamepad-driven fullscreen mode for the TV, with community themes.
Translations and hacksPatches a game you own into a translated or hacked copy without touching the original.

Install

Debian / Ubuntu

sudo apt install ./romforge_<version>_amd64.deb

Fedora / openSUSE

sudo dnf install ./romforge-<version>-1.x86_64.rpm

Arch / Manjaro

# romforge-bin PKGBUILD from the release
makepkg -si

AppImage

chmod +x romforge_<version>_amd64.AppImage
./romforge_<version>_amd64.AppImage

Install RetroArch (with a few cores) and any standalone emulators you like. romforge finds them on its own, Flatpaks too.

Getting started

  1. Open romforge. On first run it offers to make a RomForge folder for your library, games, art, saves and BIOS.
  2. In DATs, download the game lists for your systems.
  3. Scan a folder. Each game shows as verified, candidate or unknown. Nothing changes yet.
  4. Use Search to name what wasn't recognised, and Enrich to fetch art.
  5. Rename & Cleanup shows its plan, then files your verified games. Undo puts everything back.
  6. Press Play, or Fullscreen (F11) and pick up a pad.

Community themes

The fullscreen mode takes themes from romforge-themes: console-inspired looks such as NES, SNES, N64, GameCube, Game Boy, Genesis, Saturn, Dreamcast and PlayStation, plus layouts like grid, cover flow, wheel, shelves and bookshelf. Get them from Settings → Display → Get themes…
