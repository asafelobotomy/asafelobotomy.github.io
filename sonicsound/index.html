SonicSound

Album-centred music client for Navidrome, Airsonic and other Subsonic servers.

Download v0.2.4Source on GitHubSupport

What it does

A maintained, modernised fork of SonicLair. Browse by artist and album, play whole albums, radio from similar songs, lyrics when your server has them, and an Android TV build you can drive from your phone.

Screenshots

Screenshots coming soon. Until then, the README on GitHub has the full tour.

Features

Album-centredBrowse by artist and album; play whole records.
Android TVLeanback UI, phone remote, Jukebox collections, mDNS discovery.
Radio & lyricsSimilar-song radio and getLyrics support.
Works with older serversNegotiates API versions for older Airsonic / Subsonic.
